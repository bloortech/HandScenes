🦴 Anatomy Atlas

A real human skeleton, in 3D. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan — and click any bone to find out what it is and what it does.

144 individually named bones. Then hit LIVE MOTION to turn the camera on itself: your own bones, joints, skull and muscles named on you as you move. The camera is off until you ask for it, and tracking runs entirely on your device.

Heads up: the anatomical model is a ~48 MB download, so the first load can take a while on a slow connection. It is cached afterwards.
