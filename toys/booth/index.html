HOW TO PLAY

Point at any control and the bar along the top says, in plain words, what it does. Nothing here can break — try things.

Click to work the three left/top decks. Only the DJ pad reads your hands: wave a hand around it and the whole mix changes.

A first go: pick a beat with GROOVE, hold a chord pad, then sweep your hand across the DJ pad. That's a track.

DRUMS each row is one drum, each column a beat. Lit squares play.

CHORDS click a pad to hold it; it keeps ringing while you do other things. All eight fit the key, so none can sound wrong.

VOICE MIC unmutes you (already permitted at the start screen — no prompt). Push GAIN until the meter moves, TUNE pulls you onto the right notes, REC counts in a bar then loops the next two. Wear headphones or it will howl.

Keys: space play/stop, r reset the filter, x clear the drums.
