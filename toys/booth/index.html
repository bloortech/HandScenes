HOW TO PLAY

Your index fingertip is the cursor — up to two hands, one per deck. Hold still on a control to arm it; moving fast passes straight over.

DRUMS hold a cell to switch that step on or off.

CHORDS hold a pad to latch it — it keeps ringing while you go play the drums.

VOICE MIC unmutes (it was granted at the start screen, so there's no prompt), TUNE snaps your voice to the key, REC counts in a bar then captures two and loops them. Wear headphones or the mic will howl.

DJ move a hand around the pad — left/right sweeps the filter, up adds delay + reverb. It stays where you left it.

Mouse works everywhere too. Keys: space play/stop, r reset the filter, x clear the drums.
