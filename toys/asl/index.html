Learn the ASL fingerspelling alphabet with your webcam. The camera reads your handshape live and coaches you, letter by letter — hold each sign to pass, earn XP, unlock the next lesson.

Either hand works. Everything runs in your browser — no video is uploaded, nothing is recorded.

⚠ Hobby project, not a certified ASL course — made by a creative coder, not an ASL teacher, and fingerspelling is only a tiny slice of ASL (a full language with its own grammar). Handshapes were cross-checked against public references, but for real instruction please learn from Deaf teachers and Deaf-led resources like Lifeprint (ASL University) or Handspeak.
